CATALOGUE DATA:
- Del Monte SKUs and MRP figures are from the manufacturer's revised MRP page. MRP is NOT your retailer/wholesaler selling price.
- Pansari product names and pack sizes are from the official Pansari Epicure catalogue. The manufacturer page does not publish prices; enter your approved Dreamz selling prices.
- Eveready product names and example prices are third-party retailer snapshot data. They are not your Dreamz selling prices and can vary by seller, date and offer. Confirm with your distributor and replace before publishing.
- Edit products.js. For each item, replace price with your actual approved selling price and priceLabel with "Dreamz Enterprises selling price". Use price:"" if not yet confirmed.

FREE HOSTING:
1. Create/sign into GitHub at https://github.com/
2. New repository > name dreamz-enterprises-app > Public > Create.
3. Upload all files from this folder, including assets folder, then Commit changes.
4. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > branch main > folder /(root) > Save.
5. Wait for the live URL shown in Pages settings.
6. Android: open URL in Chrome > menu > Install app / Add to Home screen.
7. iPhone: open URL in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
This is a free installable web app (PWA), not a Play Store/App Store listing.
